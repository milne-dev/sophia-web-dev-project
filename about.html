A Welcome Message from Us

Our Story

North Star Bakery started as a single wood-fired oven and a love of naturally leavened bread. Since then we've grown into a neighborhood spot where every loaf, pastry, and cake is still made by hand.

Sourcing Philosophy

We bake with organic, stone-milled flour from regional mills and source fruit, dairy, and chocolate from local farms and producers whenever possible.

Staff Highlights

Our small team of bakers and decorators starts before sunrise to make sure everything is fresh when the doors open — stop in and say hello.
